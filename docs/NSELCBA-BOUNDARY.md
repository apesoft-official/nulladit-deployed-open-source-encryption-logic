# NSELCBA boundary — NODSEL

**RESEARCH ONLY — NOT DEPLOYED.**

The Node Service Exchange Lawful Canadian Barter Attestation (NSELCBA) is a separate candidate for recording a node operator's voluntary terms acceptance and service-exchange statement. It is not encryption code, does not implement or alter the messaging protocol, and is not an encryption release.

The candidate does not establish that NULL22 is or is not a security, that a transaction qualifies as barter, or that the entered service value satisfies statutory valuation rules. Its on-chain record does not verify work, token delivery, legal identity, or human identity.

NSELCBA source and its research artifact are maintained in [PELAVIUM](https://github.com/apesoft-official/nulladit-pelavium/tree/main/research) and as a private core candidate. They do not belong in NODSEL's deployed-encryption manifest. This note does not record a deployment, establish source-to-bytecode correspondence, or change the status of any NODSEL release. NODSEL continues to cover only encryption logic with verified deployment provenance and review.
