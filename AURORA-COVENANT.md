# AURORA COVENANT — encryption provenance boundary

**STATUS: PROPOSED · NOT DEPLOYED.** This note does not establish that any encryption source or artifact is deployed.

Aurora Covenant is a proposed governance-gated release registry beneath the original eight NULLADIT contracts. It may eventually anchor exact source, artifact and manifest digests for a reviewed encryption update. A registry record alone cannot prove that source corresponds to a binary, establish cryptographic safety, or show that a node installed the binary.

NODSEL remains the record for encryption logic actually deployed and verified. Do not copy this candidate governance contract into production encryption releases or label PELAVIUM experiments as deployed. Publish a production mapping only after the required independent review, exact artifact verification, and governance approval.


## Vestigium provenance boundary

VESTIGIUM is a separate proposed accountability contract; it does not prove that encryption source matches a deployed artifact. NODSEL remains the sole reference for encryption logic actually deployed and independently verified. The [public VESTIGIUM proposal](https://github.com/apesoft-official/nulladit-pelavium/blob/main/VESTIGIUM.md) is research-only and has no data-access or decryption authority.
